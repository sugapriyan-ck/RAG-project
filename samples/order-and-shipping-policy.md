# Order & Shipping Policy

## Order Tracking
Every order is assigned a tracking number after dispatch. The customer can check order status on the Orders page under Account. Statuses: Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered.

## Delivery Timeline
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days within metro cities and 5 to 7 working days for pin codes outside metros. Orders placed before 6 PM ship the same working day; orders placed after 6 PM ship the next working day. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays ship on the next working day.

## Shipping Status Meaning
- Confirmed: payment received, order placed.
- Packed: items are being prepared at the warehouse.
- Shipped: order has left the warehouse and is with the courier partner.
- Out for Delivery: courier has the package and it will be delivered today.
- Delivered: package was handed over.

## Refund Policy
Refunds for cancelled orders are processed within 5 to 7 working days after cancellation. Refunds for returned products are processed within 7 to 10 working days after the returned product passes quality inspection at the warehouse. The refund always goes back to the original payment method. If payment was made by UPI, the refund reflects within 24 hours after processing. For card payments it can take up to 3 additional banking days.

## Cancellation Policy
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before the warehouse starts packing. Once an order moves into Packed or Shipped status it can no longer be cancelled on the app; the customer must contact support. Cancelled amounts are refunded automatically.

## Change Delivery Address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order is in Confirmed or Packed status. After the order is Shipped, the address cannot be changed. To change the address, open the order and tap "Change Address".

## Returns
Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused and in its original packaging. Some products such as perishables and personalised items are not eligible for return. Return pickup is scheduled within 24 hours, and refund is processed after inspection.

## Damaged or Wrong Item
If the package arrives damaged or the wrong item is delivered, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with an unboxing video. A replacement is shipped free of cost, or a full refund is issued, at the customer's choice.
