Welcome to the ShopEasy support desk.

How to reach us
Live chat is available 24x7. Email support replies within 4 business hours. Phone support operates Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 9 PM IST.

Typical response times
Chat: immediate, automated.
Email: within 4 business hours.
Phone: 10 to 15 minutes wait during peak hours.

What we cover
Order status, delivery delays, refunds, cancellations, address changes, returns, damaged items, payment issues, and product information.

What we do not cover
Price disputes for products purchased from third-party marketplaces, warranty claims for used products, and issues created by a customer's network or device.
